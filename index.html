About the day

Why we mark it

International Men's Day is marked every year on 19 November, around the world. This year's global theme, The Future Is Everyone, is a reminder of how much we achieve together. In the UK, the day centres on supporting men and boys, guided by three simple ideas.

Wellbeing

Making a positive difference to the health and lives of men and boys.

Support

Raising awareness of the charities that help men and boys when they need it.

Conversation

Encouraging a positive, open conversation about men, manhood and masculinity.
